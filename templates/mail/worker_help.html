Find your way around

What is Outbox?

Outbox is where you work on unsent messages. You can create a message or edit a draft available to you. It includes your own drafts and shared executive-created drafts. Saving a draft does not send an email.

Why is Inbox empty?

Inbox is an inactive feature carried over from the original app. MailSend does not download received emails. Incoming messages and replies stay in the executive’s connected email account.

What does Sent show?

Sent lists messages marked sent in your workspace. Use it to look up previous messages. Sent messages cannot be edited. If a correction is needed, prepare a new draft for the executive.

What is Signature?

Signature is the shared closing added when messages are sent. A change affects future messages across the workspace, so agree on changes with your executive. You do not need to add the same signature again inside every draft.

What does Mail merge do?

Mail merge uses a CSV file and a message template to prepare personalized drafts for multiple recipients. Follow the column and placeholder instructions on that page and review the preview before creating drafts. The executive still controls sending.

Typing a placeholder into a normal message does not personalize it automatically. Use the mail merge workflow for that.

Where do I find drafts created from a document?

The executive uploads the document. Open Review imported drafts, also linked from Outbox, to complete the extracted messages. “Needs attention” shows incomplete drafts; use the other filters to see ready or all available imported drafts.

Complete a draft

How do I choose which account sends a message?

Choose From when composing or editing a draft, reviewing an imported draft, or preparing a mail merge. The executive connects the available Google accounts under Sending accounts. Check From before saving. Only the executive can send. If an account is disconnected, ask the executive to reconnect it or choose another approved sender. The shared signature is appended to messages from every account.

What should I check before saving?

Check the recipient’s identity and email address, To/CC/BCC, subject, full message body, send date, and attachments. For imported messages, compare the draft with the original document. Add any missing information, then select Save. A document mentioning an attachment does not attach that file automatically.

Why is an email address blank or only suggested?

A name may have no matching contact, several matches, or conflicting information. Similar spellings are suggestions for you to review. A unique exact contact match can fill a missing address, but you should still confirm it belongs to the intended person.

Select a suitable suggestion or enter the confirmed email address and save. Leave unknown details blank for review; do not invent an address. The executive grants Google contacts access during signup and manages the connected contacts. Workers do not need a Google connection.

What does “Needs attention” or “Ready” mean?

“Needs attention” means required information is missing. Open the draft and complete it. “Ready” means the required fields are present; it does not prove the wording or recipient is correct, and it does not mean the email has been sent.

Does the send date schedule an automatic email?

No. The date helps organize drafts. Only the executive can send. The executive can send an individual message immediately, including one with a future date.

Do I need to submit my work for approval?

Save your changes. There is no separate Submit for approval step. The executive can open the saved draft, edit it if needed, and send it. Your edits remain in the audit history.

Can I edit or delete an executive’s draft?

You can view and help edit shared executive-created drafts, including their content and attachments. You cannot delete those drafts or send any messages. Other workers’ private drafts may not be available to you.

When something needs attention

What if saving fails or someone else changed the draft?

Read the error before continuing. Keep a copy of any unsaved writing, reopen the latest draft, and compare changes before saving again. If an address is invalid, enter a confirmed address or leave it blank while you seek clarification.

How do I sign in or change my password?

Use the username and password your executive provided. Worker accounts use password sign-in. Open your account menu and choose Account security to change your password. If you cannot sign in or forgot it, ask your executive to reset it.

What if I cannot find a draft or I am unsure about a recipient?

Check your Outbox filters, Review imported drafts, and Sent. Confirm you are using the right account. If the message is still missing or its details are unclear, ask your executive before changing it.
