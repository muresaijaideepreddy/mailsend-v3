MailSend

Prepare email together. Let your executive review and send.

MailSend gives a team a shared workspace for drafting messages, preparing personalized drafts from a CSV, adding attachments, and keeping a sent history. Messages are sent through the executive's connected Gmail account after the executive takes a send action.

Sign in to MailSend Read our privacy policy

One executive, a team of assistants

Each workspace starts with one executive, who adds their assistants, reviews the workspace's drafts, and controls sending. Other executives create their own separate workspaces.

Assistants prepare drafts using accounts provided by their executive. They can view their own unsent messages and the workspace's shared Sent history. Sent messages, recipient details, and attachments are visible to members of that workspace.

Google sign-in and Gmail sending

New executives can create a MailSend account with Google. Assistants sign in only with the username and password provided by their executive. No assistant email address is required.

Google sign-in is available only to executives and uses basic identity information. Executives are asked separately for permission to send email through Gmail. MailSend does not request access to read your Gmail inbox or change Gmail settings.

Sending stays under executive control

Send dates help organize work; they do not schedule automatic delivery. An executive must review and send a message or approve a group of messages. A delivery marked as needing checking should be checked in Gmail before any further attempt.
