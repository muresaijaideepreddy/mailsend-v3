Privacy policy

This policy describes how the hosted MailSend service handles information when you sign in, prepare messages, or connect Gmail. Contact the MailSend operator at saijaideepreddymure@tamu.edu with privacy questions or data requests.

Information we collect and use

  • Account information: your username, email address, name when provided, workspace membership, and role. For local sign-in, MailSend stores a password hash rather than your readable password.
  • Google identity: your verified Google email address, account identifier, and first name when used to create an account. We use these to identify you and link the correct MailSend account. MailSend does not collect your Google password.
  • Google authorization: for an executive who connects Gmail sending, we store access and refresh tokens and related authorization information. These allow MailSend to send approved messages without asking you to reconnect on every visit. Assistants use username and password sign-in. Previously stored assistant email addresses and Google identity records may remain in account history, but Google sign-in for assistants is disabled.
  • Workspace content: recipients, including To, CC, and BCC; subjects; message bodies; signatures; uploaded attachments; and draft information prepared from CSV uploads. We store planning dates, message status, delivery identifiers, errors, and audit history to support review and delivery tracking.
  • Session information: essential cookies and server-side session records support sign-in, request security, approval steps, and temporary CSV previews. The service does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

We use this information to provide account access, workspace collaboration, executive review, Gmail delivery, support, and service security. Planning a send date does not cause an automatic send.

Google permissions and Limited Use

MailSend requests basic Google identity information for executive sign-in. Gmail sending permission is requested for the executive's connection. MailSend does not request Gmail inbox-reading or settings permissions and does not retrieve your existing Gmail messages.

MailSend's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Who can see information

Your executive can access all messages in your workspace. Assistants can access their own unsent messages, unsent messages created by their executive, and all messages in the workspace's Sent history, including recipient details and attachments. Assistants can edit their own drafts and executive-created drafts, including attachments. Only the executive can delete executive-created drafts or send messages. Draft edits are recorded in the audit history and require a new send approval. This includes BCC addresses in the stored workspace record. Other workspaces cannot access these records through the application.

When the executive sends, MailSend passes the message, addressed recipients, and attachments to Google for delivery. The addressed recipients receive the email and may retain or forward it. Google also processes sign-in and authorization requests under its own privacy policy.

The hosted service uses PythonAnywhere to run the application and store service data. Hosting and Google services process technical request information, which may include IP addresses and request metadata. See PythonAnywhere's privacy policy for its handling of information.

The MailSend operator has administrative access to stored service data. Human access to information received from Google APIs is limited to circumstances permitted by Google's Limited Use requirements, such as your permission for support, security needs, or legal obligations.

Storage and safeguards

The hosted service uses HTTPS and account and workspace permissions to protect access. Google authorization credentials are encrypted in the application database. Message content and attachments are not separately encrypted by the application; access is protected by application permissions and hosting controls. Attachments are served through authenticated downloads.

Only essential service cookies are used by MailSend. Blocking these cookies can prevent sign-in and other account actions. The application does not load third-party analytics, advertising scripts, or external fonts.

Disconnecting Google

An executive can choose Disconnect Google from the account menu. This removes the locally stored Google API access and refresh tokens and disables that connection for future sends. It retains your Google sign-in identity, MailSend account, workspace, messages, attachments, and audit history.

Disconnecting in MailSend does not revoke authorization in your Google Account. To remove Google's authorization, use your Google Account's third-party connections. Revoking access does not delete information already stored in MailSend or recall email already sent.

Retention and deletion requests

MailSend does not automatically delete workspace records after a fixed period. Authorized users can delete editable draft or failed messages; deletion also requests removal of their stored attachments. Related audit records remain. Sent messages, accounts, and entire workspaces do not currently have a self-service deletion option.

For access, correction, or deletion of account or workspace information, email saijaideepreddymure@tamu.edu. The operator will verify your identity and authority over the requested data and handle the request manually, explaining any information that must be retained. Do not include passwords or Google tokens in your request.

Removing information from MailSend does not remove delivered copies held by Google or recipients. Those services and recipients control their own copies.
